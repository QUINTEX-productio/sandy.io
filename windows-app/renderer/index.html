How to Add a Device

1. Power On Your Sensor
Plug in your Sandy / PostureAI ESP32-S3 module via USB-C. Wait until the status LED blinks cyan (BLE Pairing Mode).
2. Turn On Windows Bluetooth
Ensure Bluetooth is enabled in Windows Settings so Sandy can discover PostureAI or Sandy beacons.
3. Click the Red "Add Device" Button
Hit Scan Nearby Bluetooth Devices and select your sensor. Once status shows Connected, it automatically appears inside the Choose device dropdown on the Interface tab!
4. Manage Anytime via the Top-Left (⋮) Menu
Click the three-dot menu on the top-left of any device bar to view detailed hardware Preferences (model type, manufacture date, firmware), Rename, or Delete it.
